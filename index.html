Privacy Policy

ChuChu

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1The short version

ChuChu is a simple, family-friendly arcade game. It does not collect, store or share any personal information. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

No account required No data collected No third-party sharing No ads

2What the app accesses

Motion sensor. The game reads your device’s accelerometer while you play so you can tilt to steer. The readings are used only in the moment to move the character — they are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Sharing. Only if you tap “Share with friends,” the app creates a picture of your score card on your device and hands it, with a short message and a link to the game, to the app you pick from your phone’s share menu (for example WhatsApp). Nothing is sent unless you choose to share, and we never receive a copy.

The app needs no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo-library access.

3What the app stores

The app saves one thing locally on your device, using standard on-device storage:

  • Your best score

It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app removes it.

4What we don't do

  • We don't collect analytics or usage tracking.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't offer in-app purchases.
  • We don't require or use a login/account.
  • We don't sell or share any data, because none is collected.

5Children's privacy

ChuChu is suitable for players of all ages, including children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children either, and it contains no ads, tracking or links that let a child contact other people. Sharing a score only happens through the device’s own share menu when the player taps “Share with friends.”

6Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds ads or analytics — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
